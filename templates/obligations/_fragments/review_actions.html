{% load i18n %} {% comment %} Prepare → review → client sign-off → file, for whoever works that way. The solo tracker never sees this: someone who may mark work done without review keeps the single "is it done?" question above, and the chain stays out of their way until somebody else puts an obligation into it. Everybody else — a compliance manager, a department user, a firm's staff and managers — has no other route to a filing, so these are their next steps. Two approvals that must not be confused, and are labelled so they cannot be: the reviewer's (the preparing side checking its own work — "Approve for filing", "Approve and send for client sign-off") and the client's ("Sign off as the client"), which only the business can give.
