Somebody who answered ten of these during setup should recognise the eleventh. One form per question rather than one form with several fields: each answer is recorded and the calendar rebuilt on its own, so a person can answer the one they are sure of and leave the rest. "Not sure yet" stays available and stays distinct from "no" — an unticked box would reach the engine as a definite denial and silently remove obligations nobody ruled out. {% endcomment %}
