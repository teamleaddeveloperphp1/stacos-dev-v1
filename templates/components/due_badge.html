{% load i18n %} {% comment %} A due date with its countdown, and — when a government notification has moved it — the original date struck through beside it. That last part is not decoration. Indian due dates are extended by notification several times a year, and a client needs to see both what the statute said and what it now says, with the notification reference behind it. Building it into the component means every surface shows it consistently. `days` is signed: negative means overdue. It is computed server-side against the entity's jurisdiction, never in the browser — a client whose clock or timezone differs must not see a different answer from the one the platform will act on.
