6. {% translate "Assisting you" %}

{% blocktranslate %} The product is built so that you can answer access, correction and erasure requests yourself, and export what you need without asking us. 7. {% translate "Breach notification" %}

{% blocktranslate %} We notify you without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting your customer data, with what happened, which data is involved as far as we know it, what we are doing, and what we recommend you do. Further information follows as the investigation proceeds; we will not wait for a complete picture before telling you. {% endblocktranslate %}

8. {% translate "Return and deletion" %}

{% blocktranslate %} You may export customer data at any time during the term and for thirty days after it ends. We delete customer data ninety days after termination, or sooner on your written instruction, except where a law requires us to retain it — in which case we tell you what we are keeping and why. Backups age out on their own retention cycle, and remain protected until they do. {% endblocktranslate %}

9. {% translate "Audit" %}

{% blocktranslate %} We make available the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this addendum, including our security documentation and, for enterprise customers, a penetration test summary and a completed security questionnaire. Where that is genuinely insufficient, an audit may be conducted once a year on reasonable notice, during business hours, under confidentiality, and in a way that does not put other customers' data at risk. {% endblocktranslate %}

10. {% translate "Location and transfers" %}

{% blocktranslate %} Customer data for Indian customers is stored and processed in an Indian region.
