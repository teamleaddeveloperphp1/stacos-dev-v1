{% translate "Two different roles, and why it matters" %}

{% blocktranslate %} We handle personal data in two distinct capacities, and your rights differ between them. {% endblocktranslate %}

  • {% blocktranslate %} As a data fiduciary, for the people who deal with us directly: account users, visitors to this website, and people who contact us. This policy describes that. {% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate %} As a data processor, for the data our customers put into their accounts — their employees, directors and clients. There, the customer decides what is collected and why; we act on their instructions under the data processing addendum. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate %} If you are an employee of a STACOS customer and want to exercise rights over data held in their account, ask them: we cannot act on that data without their instruction, and telling you so is not evasion — it is the boundary that keeps their records safe from anyone claiming to be you. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "What we collect, as a fiduciary" %}

  • {% translate "Account data: name, work email, mobile number, role, organisation, and the password hash — never the password." %}
  • {% translate "Authentication data: verification attempts, trusted devices, sessions, and the addresses they came from." %}
  • {% translate "Billing data: billing name and address, GSTIN, invoices, and payment references. Card details are handled by the payment gateway; we never see or store a full card number." %}
  • {% translate "Usage data: which pages were requested and when, and diagnostic logs, for security and reliability." %}
  • {% translate "Enquiry data: whatever you send us through the contact form or by email." %}

{% translate "Why we hold it" %}

  • {% translate "To provide the service you asked for, and to bill for it." %}
  • {% translate "To verify that you are who you say you are — the codes to your email and mobile exist for that alone." %}
  • {% translate "To keep the service secure, to detect abuse, and to keep an audit record we are contractually and statutorily expected to keep." %}
  • {% translate "To answer you when you contact us." %}
  • {% translate "To tell you about material changes to the service, its terms or its security. These are service messages, not marketing." %}

{% blocktranslate %} We send marketing email only where you have asked for it, and every marketing message carries a working unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing never affects service messages. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Who else sees it" %}

{% blocktranslate %} Only the sub-processors listed on the sub-processors page, each for a defined purpose, and authorities where we are legally compelled — in which case we tell you unless we are prohibited from doing so. We do not sell personal data, we do not share it with advertising networks, and we do not use it to train machine-learning models. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Where it is stored, and for how long" %}

  • {% translate "Data for Indian customers is stored in an Indian region." %}
  • {% translate "Account data is kept while your account is active." %}
  • {% translate "Customer data is deleted ninety days after your subscription ends, unless you ask for it sooner or a law requires longer." %}
  • {% translate "Invoices and tax records are kept for the period Indian tax law requires." %}
  • {% translate "Security and audit logs are kept for twelve months, then deleted." %}

{% translate "How it is protected" %}

{% blocktranslate %} Encryption in transit and at rest, additional field-level encryption for identifiers and credentials, dual-channel verification on new devices, re-authentication on sensitive actions, and an append-only audit trail. The security page describes the controls in the detail a security team wants. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Your rights" %}

{% blocktranslate %} You may ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it where no legal obligation requires us to keep it, withdraw consent where we relied on consent, and complain to us or to the relevant authority. The DPDP notice explains how to exercise each of these and how long we take. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Children" %}

{% blocktranslate %} The service is for businesses and professional firms. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under eighteen. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Breach notification" %}

{% blocktranslate %} If personal data we hold is compromised, we will notify affected customers and the relevant authority without undue delay, with what we know, what we are doing about it, and what you should do. We would rather tell you early with an incomplete picture than late with a tidy one. {% endblocktranslate %}
