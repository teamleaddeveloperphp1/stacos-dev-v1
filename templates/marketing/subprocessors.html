{% translate "Transfers outside India" %}

{% blocktranslate %} Customer data is stored in an Indian region. The single exception is international card payments, where billing details are processed by a payment provider outside India because that is where the card networks settle. No compliance record, document or audit row leaves the country. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "How each one is assessed" %}

{% blocktranslate %} Before a vendor processes customer data we assess what it will receive, whether that is the minimum it needs, where it stores it, how it is deleted on termination, and what its own breach notification commitment is. The answers are held with the contract and are available to enterprise customers under the addendum. {% endblocktranslate %}
