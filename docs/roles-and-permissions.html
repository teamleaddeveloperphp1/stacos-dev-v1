STACOS Roles & Permissions

Who can do what in STACOS, and where. Written for the person deciding whom to invite, and as what.

The role: what can they do? Nine built-in roles, three for each kind of workspace. A role is a named set of permissions and nothing more.
The reach: where can they do it? Which entities, or which clients. Set per person, separately from the role, and it never widens because of the role.

The roles

Business

The organisation that owes the compliance. Its people prepare, check and sign off; it decides which firm may work on which of its entities.

Owner / Director

A director, proprietor or partner of the business.

Answerable for the business: signs off filings as the client and manages people, access and billing. May mark work done without review, which is recorded as such.

Compliance Manager

The in-house accountant, company secretary or compliance lead.

Runs the compliance calendar day to day: prepares, reviews colleagues' work, and records filings once approved. Cannot sign off as the client, skip review, or change who has access.

Department User

A plant, HR or admin colleague who owns a few specific compliances.

Prepares and evidences the compliances in their own areas — fire, safety, labour, licences — and submits them for review. Sees no amounts, and nothing outside their areas.

Starts limited to: Safety and fire, Labour and employment, Environment, Licensing
Viewer

A statutory auditor, investor, or board observer.

Read-only access to the calendar and the compliance profile, for auditors and observers. Cannot comment or change anything, and sees no amounts.

Professional firm

A CA or CS firm working for clients. On a client's entity a firm member may do what their role allows and the client's engagement allows — never more than either.

Partner

A partner or proprietor of the CA / CS firm.

Answerable for the firm: the whole client book, reviews, utilisation, work in progress and billing. May mark work done without review where the client allows it, recorded as such.

Manager

A senior or manager who owns a set of clients.

Owns a set of clients: assigns work, reviews staff's work, sends it for the client's sign-off and records filings. Cannot skip review or reopen a closed filing.

Staff / Article

An article clerk, assistant or junior accountant.

Prepares the work assigned to them and submits it for review, asks clients for information, and logs their own time. Sees no amounts.

Dealer

A channel partner that sells and supports subscriptions. It never sees a client's compliance, financial or document data.

Dealer Principal

The owner of the reseller business.

Manages the client accounts the dealer has sold, its own team, and its commission ledger. Sees no client compliance data.

Dealer Staff

A sales or support person at the reseller.

Onboards and supports the dealer's client accounts. Sees no client compliance data and no commission.

Everyone who can sign in can also: Change your notification preferences; Manage your own devices and sessions; See your own notifications; Use search and the command palette; View and edit your own profile; View entities; View organisation settings; View the compliance catalog; View the compliance profile.

How a filing moves

Preparing, checking and signing off are separate steps held by separate permissions, so the person answerable for a filing is not the person who prepared it.

  1. Prepare

    Do the work and submit it for review.

    Owner / Director, Compliance Manager, Department User, Partner, Manager, Staff / Article

  2. Review

    A colleague — never the preparer — checks it and approves it for filing, or sends it for the client's sign-off.

    Owner / Director, Compliance Manager, Partner, Manager

  3. Client sign-off

    When the firm asks for it, the business signs off in its own name.

    Owner / Director

  4. Record filing

    Enter the submission date and acknowledgement number, then close.

    Owner / Director, Compliance Manager, Partner, Manager

  • The checker is never the maker. Whoever submitted work for review cannot approve it or sign it off as the client. They can still take it back for changes.
  • A firm's review and the client's sign-off are different acts. A firm reviews its own work; only the business signs off in its own name. No firm role and no engagement can grant the client's sign-off.
  • Sensitive steps ask you to re-confirm who you are. These are: signing off, recording a filing, marking one done without review, reopening one, and changing someone's access.
The one exception: mark done without review. For a sole owner, or a filing made outside STACOS, Owner / Director and Partner can record a filing from wherever it stands without a review. It is a deliberate exception. The timeline and the audit trail both label it completed without review.

Reach: where a role applies

Reach is chosen when someone is invited and can be changed later by anyone who can change access. Both changes go in the audit trail with the before and after.

In a business

  • Entities: all of them, including ones added later, or a named few (e.g. one factory).

A Department User is limited to Safety and fire, Labour and employment, Environment, Licensing by their role.

In a firm

  • Clients: the whole client book, or a named few.
  • Each client's engagement: the entities, the areas and the most that may be done for that client. It caps the role and never adds to it.

In a dealer

No compliance reach at all. A dealer manages the accounts it sold and its commission.

Limits add up, and the narrowest one wins. A firm member limited to two clients, working for a client that engaged the firm for GST only, sees that client's GST only, and only on its engaged entities.

What a firm may do for a client

A client engages a firm for named entities, named compliance areas, a start date and an end date. Access stops the day an engagement ends, not whenever a job next runs.

Never, whatever the engagement says

  • Sign off a filing as the client
  • The client's people, roles and access
  • The client's billing and subscription
  • The client's registrations, organisation settings and entity list
  • The client's other engagements, and exporting its data

Dealers never see client compliance data

A dealer sells and supports subscriptions. Neither dealer role holds any compliance, financial or document permission. Naming a client on a commission row is not access to that client. If a client ever wants its dealer to see its compliance, that is a separate, time-limited engagement the client approves, like any firm's.

Glossary

Workspace
A business, a professional firm or a dealer. Everyone belongs to one or more.
Entity
A company, LLP, partnership or branch that owes compliance. A business can have several.
Client
A business, as seen by the firm working for it.
Engagement
A client's permission for a firm to work on named entities and compliance areas, for a period.
Role
What someone can do: a named set of permissions.
Reach
Where they can do it: entities, or clients.
Review
The preparing side checking its own work before filing. Never done by the preparer.
Client sign-off
The business approving a filing in its own name. Only a business can give it.
Completed without review
A filing recorded without anyone checking it. Only an Owner or a Partner can do this, and it is labelled on the record.
Audit trail
An append-only record of every change: who, when, from where, before and after.
